Delete Account

Google Play requirement — in-app and website account deletion

How to delete your PATH SSC account

You can delete your account and all associated data in two ways:

  1. In the app: go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm.
  2. By email: send a deletion request to mistan44op@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion" and the email address you registered with.

What gets deleted

  • Account credentials and profile information.
  • Practice data, quiz results, bookmarks and preferences.
  • Any files you uploaded (e.g. avatars).

What is retained

We may keep a minimal record for legal or security obligations (e.g. fraud prevention), but this never includes your personal content. Aggregate, anonymised statistics are not linked to you.

Processing time

Deletion is processed within 30 days of your verified request. You will receive a confirmation email when it is complete.

Questions?

Contact us at mistan44op@gmail.com
