Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the PATH SSC app ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses and protects your information when you use our service for government exam preparation (SSC GD, CGL, CHSL, MTS, Railway NTPC / Group D).

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and a user ID when you register.
  • Usage data: quiz results, practice activity and in-app preferences used to personalise your experience.
  • Optional location: approximate region may be used to show relevant government job notifications. This is optional and never required.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide core features: notes, mock tests, quizzes, job updates, admit-card alerts and AI-assisted practice.
  • To improve the app and support users.

4. Advertising

The app displays ads from third-party providers (Adsterra). These providers may use device identifiers to show ads consistent with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to ad networks.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is stored on secured servers (Supabase) with row-level security, so each user can only access their own records. We may share information only when required by law.

6. Data Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and follow standard security practices. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email on our Contact page. We will delete your data within a reasonable period.

8. Children

The app targets users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy in the app and on this page.

10. Contact
