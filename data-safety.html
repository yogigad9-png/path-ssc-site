Data Safety

As declared in the Google Play data-safety form

Data Collected

  • Email address — for account creation and login.
  • User IDs — to associate your practice data with your account.
  • Approximate location (optional) — to show relevant regional job notifications.
  • App usage & diagnostics — to improve features and fix issues.

How Data Is Used

  • Account management and authentication.
  • Core app functionality (notes, quizzes, jobs, alerts).
  • Personalisation and app improvement.

Data Sharing

  • Not sold — we never sell personal data.
  • Ads are served by third-party networks (Adsterra) under their own policies.
  • We share data only as required by law.

Security

  • Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Row-level security on the database — users access only their own records.
  • Account deletion available on request (see Contact).

Declaring for Google Play

This page mirrors the answers given in the Play Console data-safety form: data is collected, not sold, encrypted in transit, and the app is not designed for children under 13.
